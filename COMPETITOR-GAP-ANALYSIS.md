### We are now ahead on one thing, and it is worth leading with

Solution Roofing's "instant estimator" — and every other one in the market —
is a contact form with a progress bar. It asks for your details and emails
you. It does not measure anything.

Ours measures the roof. Three ways:

1. **Address lookup** against the national property records (~120ms).
2. **Trace it yourself on an aerial photograph** of your own house, using LINZ
   imagery. The number becomes *yours* — you chose it, so you trust it, and if
   the automatic lookup got it wrong you have already fixed it rather than
   argued with it.
3. **Describe the house** — bedrooms, storeys, era — for anyone who would
   rather not do either.

It then explains, from your own answers, exactly what will move your price.
That is a genuinely better answer to "how much will this cost" than a per-m²
range, and it is defensible without a single figure from Eugene.

### Built since the last review

- A page per service, and a services hub (was: one homepage list of twelve).
- A "what happens when you call" timeline, on every service page.
- Emergency roofing given its own page and its own path through the site.
- The full gallery on its own page, filtered by the work shown.
- A roof cost guide that answers the question honestly without a price.
- Photo galleries per service, drawn from the verified job photographs.

### Still ahead of us, and still blocked on Eugene

   to show is worse than no slider. One "before" photo unlocks it.
3. **Case studies with real suburbs.**
4. **A credentials row** — licensing, insurance, memberships, guarantee.
5. **Finance options.** Solution Roofing doesn't offer these either; no
   evidence the market expects it. Not recommended.

## The one-line summary

Solution Roofing is not beating us on design. It is beating us on **proof** and
on **structure**: it can show a real Google rating, real credentials and a real
guarantee, and it has a separate page for every service and every city so
Google has something to rank. We have neither yet — but only the first of those
needs Eugene. The second we can build ourselves.

## Blocked on Eugene — cannot be built honestly without him

### 1. Google reviews, with the real rating and count 🔴 highest value of anything on this list
Solution shows **4.9★ from 120+ Google reviews** on the homepage, in the header
area, and again near every quote button. For a trade, this is the single
strongest conversion element that exists — more than photos, more than copy.
We have one written review (Nolan L.) and nothing else.

**Needs:** the Google Business Profile link, and permission to quote the
reviews. Already logged in LAUNCH-BLOCKERS.md #3c. **Ask for this first.**

### 2. A credentials row
Solution lists: Roofing Association of NZ member, Licensed Building
Practitioner, Site Safe member, QBE-insured with a stated $2M public liability
cover, and Colorsteel Approved Installer. Every one of those is a badge a
homeowner scans for before ringing.

**Needs:** LAUNCH-BLOCKERS.md #4, all ten questions. Not one of them may go on
the site until confirmed — this codebase has already invented a guarantee once.

### 3. A stated workmanship guarantee
Solution states 5 years, plainly, with a page explaining it. This is usually the
last thing a customer checks before choosing between two quotes.

**Needs:** the real figure from Eugene, in writing. Nothing goes up until then.

### 4. Before-and-after photo pairs
The most persuasive image a roofer can publish, and Solution leans on it hard
(they also have an interactive visualiser). We have plenty of *during* and
*after* photos and no *before* shots at all.

**Needs:** Eugene to send the "before" photo for any job we already have an
"after" of. Costs him nothing — they will be on his phone.

### 5. "Est. 20xx" and a jobs-completed figure
Solution leads with "Est. 2014". A founding year is cheap credibility and we
cannot state one.

**Needs:** LAUNCH-BLOCKERS.md #4.

### 6. A response-time promise
Solution publishes "Avg. response · 3 hrs". Powerful next to an emergency
number, and completely unusable unless it is true.

**Needs:** Eugene to say what he can actually commit to.

### 7. Insurance-claim help
Storm-damage claims are a major roofing lead source and Solution answers it in
their FAQ. We say nothing.

**Needs:** Eugene to confirm whether he works with insurers and how.

### 8. Suburb and region landing pages
Solution has separate pages for Auckland, Hamilton and Wellington and ranks in
each. The equivalent for us is suburb pages — "Roofing in Onehunga", "Roofing
on the North Shore".

**Partly blocked:** the page structure can be built now, but each page must
name suburbs Hynson genuinely covers and, ideally, show a real job there.
**Needs:** Eugene to confirm the suburb list and which photos belong to which
suburb (already on the list as LAUNCH-BLOCKERS.md #3b).

## Buildable now, with no invented facts

### 9. A separate page for every service 🔴 biggest structural gap
Solution has a page per service. We have one homepage listing twelve services,
which means Google has nothing to rank for "roof repairs Auckland",
"re-roofing Auckland", "membrane roofing Auckland" and so on. **A homepage
listing twelve services ranks for none of them.**

Every one of the twelve descriptions already exists in the client's own words on
his live site, so this needs nothing from him. This is the largest amount of
value available to us without asking Eugene a single question, and it is the
work that has just been commissioned separately.

### 10. A visible "how it works" process
Solution shows a four-step diagram. Ours is genuinely known and confirmed from
the client's own FAQ: **get in touch → free site inspection → written quotation
→ the work → sign-off.** No timeframes attached, because none are confirmed.

### 11. A proper cost guide page
Solution publishes "$120–$180 per m²" and a "$15k–$25k" worked example. We
cannot publish figures — but we can publish a page about **what moves the
price**, which the calculator already lists (condition of the substrate, access
and storey height, roof shape and flashings, extras done at the same time). A
genuinely useful page that ranks for "how much does a new roof cost NZ" and
does not require a single invented number.

### 12. Educational content: roofing profiles, materials, colours
Solution has "How Roofing Works", "Roofing Profiles" and "Colour Range". These
are general industry facts, not claims about Hynson, so they are safe to write —
they demonstrate expertise and they rank. ("Brands we work with" is *not* safe:
that is a claim about Hynson's supplier relationships and needs Eugene.)

### 13. A full gallery page with filters
We have a good pinned gallery on the homepage showing eight photos. Solution
shows 60+ projects with location tags on a page of its own. Ours can move to a
real page with filters and hold everything Eugene ever sends, instead of being
capped by what fits on a homepage.

### 14. A footer that works as a sitemap
Solution's footer mirrors the whole site. Ours lists five links. Once there are
real pages, the footer is how Google and customers find all of them.

### 15. Breadcrumbs on inner pages
Standard on every site that ranks, and currently impossible because there are no
inner pages.

## Considered and deliberately not recommended

- **A "why us" comparison table.** Solution has one. Ours would have to be
  filled with claims we cannot verify, and a comparison table full of vague
  ticks is worse than nothing.
- **An 0800 number.** Solution has one; it costs money monthly, and Eugene's
  mobile is already published everywhere. Not worth raising unless he asks.
- **Financing / payment plans.** Solution doesn't offer it either. No evidence
  it is expected in this market.
- **A blog.** Two real posts exist on the Wix site but read as generic filler
  (LAUNCH-BLOCKERS.md #6). A thin blog is worse for credibility than no blog.
  The educational pages in #12 do the same SEO job far better.
